## en-US

Expand a FileBrowser directory's single-child directory chain after a user opens it. Stop at branching, files, incomplete pages, errors, or denied access; ignore stale expansion after a fold or scope change.

## zh-CN

用户展开 FileBrowser 目录后，自动逐层展开唯一子目录；遇到分支、文件、未完成分页、错误或无权限时停止，折叠及切换作用域后不再继续旧展开。
