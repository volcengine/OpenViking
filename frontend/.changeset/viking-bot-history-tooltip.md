## en-US

Return focus to the VikingBot session history button when a session dialog closes and focus would otherwise fall back to the page. The tooltip stays hidden after mouse dismissal and remains available on hover and keyboard focus.

## zh-CN

VikingBot 会话操作弹窗关闭后，如果焦点落到页面主体，会将焦点恢复到历史会话按钮。用鼠标关闭弹窗时保持提示隐藏，鼠标悬停和键盘聚焦时仍会显示提示。
