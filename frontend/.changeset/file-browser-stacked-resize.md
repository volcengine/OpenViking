## en-US

Resize the stacked FileBrowser tree by dragging the divider or pressing the up and down arrow keys when it has focus. The divider keeps at least 160px for the tree and 240px for the preview.

## zh-CN

FileBrowser 在上下布局中支持拖动分隔条，或聚焦分隔条后按上下方向键调整树高。调整时为目录树保留至少 160px，为预览区保留至少 240px。
