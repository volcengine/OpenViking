## en-US

Render VikingBot HITL forms with internal controls instead of Ark AskHuman, matching the console's confirmation cards, numbered choices, purple actions and segmented schedule frequency. Preserve time and weekday drafts across frequency changes, support the themed time panel, and retain input for retry after submission errors. Honor single-line and multiline text requests. The standalone interaction no longer requires Ark Design; existing request and answer shapes remain compatible. Export the dedicated interaction stylesheet.
