## en-US

Keep Wiki conflict highlights visible while any part of a highlighted block remains. Open the selected conflict in the editor, close conflict tooltips when scrolling or leaving the editor, and show a success notice after saving a Wiki file.

## zh-CN

编辑 Wiki 冲突时，高亮保留到整段内容被删除。点击冲突的铅笔会定位到该条冲突，滚动编辑器或焦点离开时收起冲突说明。Wiki 文件保存成功后，右上角显示成功提示。
