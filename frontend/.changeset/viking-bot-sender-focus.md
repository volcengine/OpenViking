## en-US

Clicking anywhere on the blank area of the VikingBot composer now focuses the editor with the caret at the end; buttons, inputs and links in the composer keep their own behavior. The panel shows a text cursor except over its buttons, and the attach and reference icons are larger.

## zh-CN
