## en-US

Allow hosts to open the built-in AgentConnect drawer from an external entry and observe user open and close requests through `agentConnectOpen` and `onAgentConnectOpenChange` on FileBrowser and FileBrowserActionBar.

## zh-CN

FileBrowser 和 FileBrowserActionBar 新增 `agentConnectOpen` 与 `onAgentConnectOpenChange`，宿主可从外部操作打开内置 AgentConnect 抽屉，并接收用户打开、关闭抽屉的请求。
