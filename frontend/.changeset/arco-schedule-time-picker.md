## en-US

Replace the VikingBot schedule time panel with an internal picker adapted from Arco TimePicker, including compact scrolling columns and wheel selection. Preserve HH:mm values, keyboard controls, input validation and disabled states. Keep overlapping hour/minute updates from reverting each other.
