## en-US

Replace FilePreview's textarea with a lazily loaded CodeMirror 6 editor with line numbers, undo/redo and syntax highlighting. Share Prism with FileViewer and load 44 syntax grammars on demand, including extension aliases, special filenames and MIME hints. Preserve drafts, save retries and locale changes, and keep the editing area borderless.
