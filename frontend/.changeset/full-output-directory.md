## en-US

Add the built-in VikingBot target-directory form with host-controlled search, automatic ancestor expansion and pagination, selected-directory reveal, child-directory creation, write-permission feedback and host-owned validation before submission. Restore selection when tree rows arrive asynchronously. Preserve drafts on failures and accept successful creation independently of subsequent refreshes. Existing choice-directory pickers remain supported.
