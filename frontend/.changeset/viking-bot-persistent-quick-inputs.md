## en-US

VikingBot keeps its quick inputs above the built-in composer at all times, including on the welcome screen, so they can fill the draft at any point in a conversation. The welcome screen shows them only when the host supplies its own composer.

## zh-CN

VikingBot 的快捷输入常驻在内置输入框上方，欢迎页也不例外，对话进行中随时可以填入草稿。只有宿主自带输入区时，欢迎页才显示快捷输入。
